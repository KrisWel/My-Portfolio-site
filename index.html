About

I'm a QA Automation Engineer with three years of experience testing enterprise storage and identity systems — most recently building and maintaining a ~200-test Python/Pytest/Playwright automation suite for Dell PowerStore.

I move comfortably between manual test design, automation, CI/CD ownership, and communicating quality risk to technical and non-technical stakeholders alike.
